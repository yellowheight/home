About

I am a part-time teacher and part-time writer.

I've been a climate researcher, a Microsoft engineer, a hedge fund manager, and an entrepreneur. At 45, I became a math teacher. I published a book about the experience and continue to write about education — especially math education.

I post regularly on Substack, where I share free chapters from that book as well as draft chapters for my next one.
